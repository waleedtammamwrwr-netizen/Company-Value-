Basis of Calculation

Every Headline Figure, Derived

Figure Derivation (AED M)
Total asset value Sum of 6 assets = 317.560
Company value 317.560 + 180.000 (Key Mavens) = 497.560
Value per 1% ownership 497.560 ÷ 100 = 4.976
30% interest 497.560 × 0.30 = 149.268
Income to the 30% interest 29.350 × 0.30 = 8.805
Cash yield on price 8.805 ÷ 149.268 = 5.90%
Pipeline profit Sum of 7 projects = 413.000
Path 2 company value 497.560 + 413.000 = 910.560
Path 2 · 30% interest value 910.560 × 0.30 = 273.168
Capital uplift on the 30% interest 273.168 − 149.268 = 123.900 (÷ 149.268 = +83.0% on price)

Every % here is one value ÷ another, shown beside it — yields divide income by price, margins divide profit by value, uplift divides gain by entry price: 5.90% cash yield, +83.0% uplift, 2.12× multiple, ≈17.6% IRR.
